## The year in one paragraph

Finish the cut to ~154 lb (~13% by DEXA) by mid-December. Then ~3
weeks establishing true maintenance — expect weight to RISE from
glycogen/water/food mass; **154 is NOT the long-term weight target**
(glycogen-replete maintenance likely sits several lb above it);
maintenance = the intake where the 7-day avg is ~stable for 2-3 weeks.
From there: the post-cut week (squat Mon, bench + TECHNIQUE climb Tue,
calisthenics Wed, 4x4 Thu, deadlift + LIMIT climb Fri, OHP+bench2 Sat,
rest Sun) — every main lift gets a heavy top set per the repeating
4-WEEK WAVE (wk1 top ~5, wk2 top ~3, wk3 top ~1, wk4 deload — the
block's light week IS the wave's deload) at RPE 7-8, then back-offs
3-4x5-8 @ 1-3 RIR (deadlift 2x4-6) and the concrete accessory schedule
(RDL, BSS, rows, laterals, triceps, curls, leg press, leg curl, calf
raises, face pulls, external rotations), with 8-12 productive sets per
muscle group per week counted across compounds, climbing and
calisthenics. First 2 maintenance weeks ramp accessory volume
(60-70%, then 80-90%); climbing-emphasis blocks cut lifting volume
30-40% with heavies preserved. Success metrics: strength UP, lean
mass UP, climbing UP, calisthenics UP, VO2 UP, body fat ~13%. Scale
weight itself is secondary.

## The rules that matter (post-cut, per the spec)

- Maintenance: established over 2-3 stable weeks after the BF target;
  re-found when the 7-day avg trends steadily for 3+ weeks.
- **A flat scale NEVER triggers +100 kcal** — flat with strength/lean
  mass rising is the plan working (this reverses the old
  down-0.5-adds-100 adjust step).
- Allow body weight to increase if waist/BF are stable and
  performance/lean mass are improving.
- Weight AND waist consistently up WITHOUT performance → reduce
  calories modestly.
- Before ANY +100 kcal: run the PLATEAU CHECKLIST (final spec) — rule
  out (1) inadequate volume, (2) inadequate proximity to failure,
  (3) poor progressive overload, (4) insufficient carbs, (5) poor
  sleep/recovery, (6) excessive climbing/calisthenics overlap,
  (7) inconsistent adherence. Only if ALL are consistently adequate
  and progress is still stalled: ~+100 kcal/day for one block, then
  re-check. Never a return to bulking.
- Fat gauge: weekly navel waist 7-day average (NOT TRACKED YET — open
  item) + DEXA every 3-4 months. Muscle gauge: DEXA lean mass +
  strength/hypertrophy progression + test-week singles every 8 weeks.
