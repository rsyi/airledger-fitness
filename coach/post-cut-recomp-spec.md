# Post-Cut Goal (user-authored, 2026-09-27)

Keep the current cut unchanged until ~154 lb / ~13% body fat, ideally
confirmed by DEXA. After the cut, transition to a long-term recomp
program designed to: 1. Gain muscle 2. Increase squat/bench/deadlift/
OHP strength 3. Improve rock climbing 4. Improve calisthenics skills
5. Improve/maintain VO2 max 6. Maintain ~13% body fat, rather than
intentionally bulk.

## Phase 1: Establish Maintenance
~3 weeks establishing true maintenance after the BF target. Expect
weight to rise from glycogen/water/food mass — do NOT treat 154 as the
long-term weight target; glycogen-replete maintenance likely several
lb above 154. Find the calorie level where 7-day avg is ~stable for
2-3 weeks. Use 7d avg + waist + periodic DEXA to distinguish muscle
from fat gain.

## Nutrition (~155-160 lb starting targets)
Eat ~at maintenance, not a bulk. Protein 160-175 g/day. Fat minimum
~55-65 g/day. Carbs = remaining calories, likely ~225-300 g/day.
Bias carbs toward lifting/climbing/VO2 sessions; ~50-75 g in the 1-3h
before demanding training is reasonable, no rigid timing. Goal:
sufficient protein + carbs + energy availability to maximize training
quality and muscle growth while stored fat supplies part of the
energetic cost of lean tissue.

## Hypertrophy — first-class objective
~8-12 productive sets per major muscle group per week (~10 default).
Count overlapping stimulus from compounds, climbing, and calisthenics
rather than blindly adding sets. Most hypertrophy sets ~1-3 RIR / RPE
7-9. Compounds generally stop ~2-3 RIR. Safe isolations occasionally
0-1 RIR. Increase volume only when progress stalls and recovery is
good. Particularly avoid excessive pulling volume (climbing,
muscle-ups, pull-ups, rows, curls all overlap).

## Strength
Continue Ledger's working-max/controller for the four lifts. Each
major lift gets BOTH: strength exposure (heavy 1-3 reps, generally
RPE 7-8) and hypertrophy/volume exposure (mostly 3-8 reps RPE 7-9,
enough weekly volume to contribute to the muscle group's 8-12 sets).
Post-cut training has substantially more productive volume than the
cut's minimal heavy-set+backoff. Track progressive overload in both.

## Approximate Weekly Structure
Mon: Squat heavy · lower-body hypertrophy · muscle-up skill
Tue: Bench/heavy upper · upper hypertrophy as appropriate · Climbing
Wed: Calisthenics skill · recovery/easy
Thu: Norwegian 4x4 VO2 · no additional high-fatigue training
Fri: Deadlift heavy · lower-body volume · Climbing
Sat: OHP · second bench/upper exposure · upper hypertrophy/accessories
Sun: Rest
No heavy lower-body lifting on Tuesday (conflicts with climbing).

## Climbing
Start 2 sessions/week, differentiated: technique/volume session
(easier/moderate, movement variety, onsights, deliberate technique)
vs limit session (hard V5-V7+ projects, long rests, low junk volume).
Track new V5+ sends/onsights and limit-project progress. Future blocks
may temporarily run 3x/week with reduced lifting volume.

## Calisthenics
Primarily skill training, not high-fatigue hypertrophy: muscle-up,
handstand/HSPU, front lever, pistol squat, hanging leg raise/core.
Low-fatigue high-quality practice, long rests, stop when quality
deteriorates.

## VO2 Max
At least 1 Norwegian 4x4/week; purpose is cardiovascular adaptation,
not calorie expenditure. Extra aerobic work → walking/easy Zone 2.

## Body-Fat / Calorie Control
Maintenance recomp, not a conventional bulk. Do NOT automatically
increase calories because scale weight is flat — flat scale with
increasing strength and lean mass is a SUCCESSFUL outcome. Monitor:
daily weight → 7d avg; weekly waist at navel; DEXA every 3-4 months;
strength progression; hypertrophy performance; climbing performance.
Allow body weight to increase if waist/BF stable and performance/lean
mass improving. If weight AND waist consistently rise without
performance/lean-mass improvement: reduce calories modestly. If lean
mass and strength stall despite high adherence, recovery, and
appropriate volume: consider ~+100 kcal/day, not a return to bulking.

## Core Philosophy
Adequate protein + sufficient carbs + ~8-12 productive sets/muscle/
week + training close enough to failure + progressive overload +
maintenance energy intake. Success metrics: strength UP, lean mass
UP, climbing UP, calisthenics UP, VO2 UP, body fat ~13%. Scale weight
itself is secondary.
