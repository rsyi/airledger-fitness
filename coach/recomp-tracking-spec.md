# Post-cut recomp tracking spec (user-authored, 2026-09-27)
Goal: distinguish adherence INPUTS from adaptation OUTCOMES; easy
daily log + weekly coaching review.

## DAILY INPUTS
BODY COMP: morning weight + time; optional BIA bf (don't overinterpret
daily); weight primarily feeds the 7d avg.
NUTRITION: calories, protein g, carbs g, fat g. Derived daily
adherence: protein target met Y/N; fat minimum met Y/N; carb range
met Y/N; calories vs current maintenance; on demanding days,
pre-workout carbs ~1-3h before if known. No rigid timing required.
STRENGTH/HYPERTROPHY: every working set: exercise, load, reps,
RPE-or-RIR, set type (warmup/heavy strength/hypertrophy/skill/rehab),
muscle group(s). Derived: productive hypertrophy sets by muscle
group; sets within ~0-3 RIR; heavy exposures for S/B/D/OHP;
progression vs previous comparable session. Warmups + easy skill do
NOT count as hypertrophy sets.
CLIMBING: session type (technique/volume vs limit/project), grade,
ascent type, attempts, new send vs repeat, ~duration. Derived:
highest attempted, highest sent, first-time V5+ sends, V5+
onsights/flashes, project progress where possible.
CALISTHENICS: skill, progression/variation, sets/reps or hold
duration, assistance level, technically-clean flag, optional RPE/RIR.
Primary skills: muscle-up, handstand/HSPU, front lever, pistol squat,
core. Measure skill quality/progression, not hypertrophy volume.
VO2/CARDIO (4x4): interval duration, speed/incline or workload, HR,
time to Z4, time to Z5, max HR, all-four-completed, notes. Progress =
more EXTERNAL workload at comparable/improved physiological response,
not higher HR.
RECOVERY (daily subjectives): sleep duration; sleep quality/recovery
rating if available; fatigue 1-5; soreness 1-5; any pain/tendon/joint
issue; optional motivation/readiness 1-5. Pain outranks numerical
programming targets as a coaching signal.

## WEEKLY INPUT REVIEW (generated)
NUTRITION: avg cals/day, current est. maintenance, avg protein/day,
days protein met /7, avg carbs, avg fat, days fat min met /7. The
question: did nutrition support growth + performance without
persistent excess?
HYPERTROPHY per muscle group (quads, hamstrings, glutes, chest,
delts, triceps, back/lats, biceps): productive hard sets/wk, ~avg
RIR/RPE, target range, trend vs prior weeks. Account for
climbing/calisthenics overlap. Flag: insufficient / appropriate /
potentially excessive overlapping volume.
STRENGTH weekly: per-lift exposure/performance, WM changes, estimated
trend, completion of prescribed heavy + volume exposures. One bad
session != strength loss.
CLIMBING: sessions vs target, new V5+ sends, highest sent/attempted,
onsight/flash, technique-vs-limit adherence.
CALISTHENICS: sessions, best per active skill, progression vs prior.
VO2: sessions vs target, workload, completion quality, HR response,
Z4/Z5 exposure, workload-at-comparable-HR trend.
RECOVERY: avg sleep, fatigue trend, soreness/pain flags, accumulating
systemic fatigue, is planned volume recoverable.

## WEEKLY BODY-COMP REVIEW
7d avg weight; change vs previous 7d avg; multi-week trend. Weekly
waist at navel (standardized). DEXA ~every 3-4 months. Do NOT react
to daily weight.

## WEEKLY COACHING DECISION (answer in order)
1 protein adequate? 2 calories ~maintenance? 3 carbs sufficient for
performance? 4 each muscle sufficient productive volume? 5 hypertrophy
sets close enough to failure? 6 strength progressing/on plan?
7 climbing/calisthenics/VO2 targets completed? 8 recovery adequate?
9 body fat/waist ~stable? 10 should anything actually change next
week? Prefer changing training/recovery over calories when the
problem is training/recovery. Never +calories just because weight is
flat.

## CORE DASHBOARD (one screen)
BODY: 7d avg, weekly change, waist, latest DEXA BF%/lean.
NUTRITION: avg cals/protein/carbs/fat, protein adherence %.
HYPERTROPHY: productive sets per muscle, avg proximity to failure,
exercises progressing/stalled.
STRENGTH: S/B/D/OHP trends.
SKILLS: climbing sessions + V5+ sends; calisthenics sessions + PRs.
CARDIO: 4x4 completed?, external workload trend.
RECOVERY: sleep, fatigue, pain flags.
Purpose: "Am I consistently providing the stimulus, nutrition, and
recovery required to gain muscle and strength while remaining ~13%
body fat?"
