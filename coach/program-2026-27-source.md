# Bulk Program 2026–27
Sep 18, 2026 · @Robert Yi

Finish the cut to about 154 lb (13% body fat by DEXA) by mid-December, then gain 0.4 lb a week to 165 and 0.2 a week to 170 on a four-day heavy/volume lifting week, with two or three climbing sessions and one 4x4, in eight-week blocks that alternate between a lifting emphasis and a climbing emphasis.

## How it fits together
Read it as a loop: food goes up only when lifts or sends went up, and every other signal changes training first, not food.
Flow: Cut to 154 (done by Dec 13) -> 3-week reverse diet (Dec 14 - Jan 3) -> 8-week blocks alternating climbing emphasis (3 climbs, lifts held) / lifting emphasis (lifts pushed, 2 climbs). Every week: heavy sets on every lift, bench and press twice, gain 0.2-0.5 lb. Weekly check: weight up over 0.6/wk -> eat 100 kcal less; joint pain / guarded back / bike intervals falling -> light week or drop a session, food stays; all clear week 8 -> test one single at RPE 8 per lift, count hard sends. Block's sport up -> switch emphasis, next block. Not up: sessions not delivered -> fix training same food; delivered -> a bit more food next time. Weight reaches 170 -> hold 168-170.

## Targets and rules
The whole plan lives between 154 and 170 lb, and the scale never goes above 172.
- Cut end point: ~154 lb, 13% by DEXA (12% is the floor below which fat loss turns into muscle loss)
- Gain rate 154->165: +0.4 lb/week (full program at 0.3-0.5/wk gained +43 on the total per 14 weeks)
- Gain rate 165->170: +0.2 lb/week (full program at 0.1-0.3 gained +26; best window Dec 2024 +88 at +0.25)
- Hard cap: 172 lb (nothing good above 178; every 2025 loss came at 176-186)
- Block length: 8 weeks then a light week (productive stretches delivered inside 8-10 weeks; 19 weeks in 2025 was the failure)
- Lifting days: 4/week, 2 lifts each (Nov 2024-Feb 2025, +90 total, ran this shape)
- Bench and press: twice a week each (two bench days: +35 avg, 95% of windows improved; one: +7)
- Heavy sets: 6 near-max sets/week: top set of 1-3 at RPE 8.5-9 on each heavy day, plus a single Saturday for bench and squat. Behind them ~15 sets of 3 at 82% and 9 sets of 8. Warm-ups don't count. (Every productive stretch had 6+ sets within 5% of best — Bulk C 6, Dec 2024-Feb 2025 14; every failed one 4 or fewer. The count of near-max sets mattered, not average heaviness.)
- Volume: 25-30 working sets/week on the four lifts (80%+ of best); ~45 with warm-ups. (Under 15: lost ground. 15-20: +8. 20+: +23. Warm-up count had no relationship to gains.)
- Climbing: 2 sessions in lifting blocks, 3 in climbing blocks; partner day Tuesday afternoon through June. (3-4 sessions on top of 4 lifting days broke weeks 11-19 of 2025.)
- Muscle-ups: one skill session/week, ~10 reps, Monday after squats. Maintain the Aug 19 2026 standard.
- VO2: one 4x4/week, every block, never dropped. The longevity lever.
The test that matters at the end of every block is a single at RPE 8 on each lift, not an estimate from a rep set.

## Phase 0 — finish the cut (Sep 21 - Dec 13)
163 -> 154 at current 0.7 lb/wk = 12-13 weeks, lands mid-December.
Stays: current cut routine (heavy squat/deadlift alternate weeks, top singles RPE 8, one 4x4, two climbing sessions, muscle-up day).
Changes:
- Add a second bench day. Light: 3x5 at 70% of best single, Thursday or Saturday. (2024 mini-cut kept bench 2x and held strength; 2026 cut ran bench once and lost it.)
- One maintenance week, Nov 2-8. Eat at maintenance, lift as normal.
- DEXA in that same week, at ~157 lb. The scan decides whether 13% really means 154; the end point moves to match.
- Last four weeks (Nov 16 - Dec 13): top singles at RPE 7, not 8. The back gate already tripped once (Sep 17).
- Climbing is Tuesday afternoons through June. Tuesday morning is press work or nothing. Heavy squat and deadlift never land on a Tuesday.
Expect squat and deadlift singles to lose another 3-4% by 154 (roughly 295->285, 315->305). They come back in the first two blocks.

## Phase 1 — reverse diet (Dec 14 - Jan 3)
- Food: add ~150 kcal/day each week. Stop when the 7-day average has held steady two weeks. That number is maintenance; write it down.
- Lifting: switch to the four-day week below, every top set at RPE 7. Stimulus before food; in 2025 the food arrived first.
- Climbing: three sessions, all volume/technique, no limit attempts. Finger/elbow on-ramp: bodyweight hangs on a large edge, 5x10s, start of two sessions.
- 4x4: one a week.
- Bodyweight: hold 154-155. If it climbs more than a pound, take ~150 kcal back out.

## The training week (from Jan 4)
Mon AM: Squat heavy (top 1-3 @8.5-9, then 4x3 @82%) + hanging leg raise, then muscle-ups (skill ~10 reps).
Tue AM: Bench heavy (top 1-3, then 4x3 @82%) + press volume 3x8. PM: Climb 1 limit/projecting (partner day through June). No squat or deadlift this day, ever.
Wed AM: 4x4 bike + 10 min hip/thoracic mobility. Easy day.
Thu AM: Deadlift heavy (top 1-3 @8.5-9, then 3x3 @82%) + press heavy (top 1-3, then 4x3 @82%).
Fri PM: Climb 2 board/steep power. Max hangs first in climbing blocks.
Sat AM: Bench single @8.5 then 3x8; pull power (weighted pull-ups 5x3, rows, face pulls); squat single @8.5 then 3x8. Longest session. Optional deadlift 3x5 @65% only after four clean weeks and if not running long.
Sun: Off. Climb 3 (volume/technique) climbing blocks only.
Weekly: 4 lifting days, ~46 main-lift sets incl. warm-ups: 6 near-max top sets, ~15x3 @82%, 9x8, bench 2x, squat 2x, press 2x, deadlift 1x, one muscle-up session, climbing 2-3, one 4x4.
Daily, five minutes: face pulls, external rotations, reverse wrist curls or rice bucket (elbow insurance).
Bench days Tue + Sat. If Tuesday climbing suffers after heavy bench, swap heavy to Saturday. Climbing day never has squat or deadlift in the morning.

## Loads and progression
Heavy days: 4-5 warm-ups, one top set 1-3 @8.5-9 (single ~96%, double ~90%, triple ~88% of max; rotate weekly), then 4x3 (deadlift 3x3) at 81-83%, RPE 6.5->7.5. Back-offs set off the MAX, never the top single. Working max moves on the heavy-day top set only: RPE 8 or easier +5 next session; 8.5-9 hold; 9.5 or grinder -5 now and next top set capped @8; two 9.5s in a row = no top sets that lift next week. Saturday single never raises it, only lowers. Frozen through light week, climbing blocks, reverse diet (only the drop rule runs). Test-week single resets it: WM = single / 0.92 rounded down to 5. Never a grinder.
Volume days: 3x8 @~70% of best triple; +1 rep/set weekly to 3x10, then +5 lb back to 3x8.
Deadlift Thursday only. Press: heavy Thursday, volume Tuesday.
Muscle-ups Monday after squats: 3-4 sets of 1-2 at standard, 2x3-5 banded strict, front lever up-downs 2x5. No progression target this year.
Light week (wk 4): top set @6, no back-offs, volume 2x8, one climb fewer, hangboard off.
Test week (wk 8): single @8 on squat/bench/deadlift/press. Those four numbers are the block's result.
Lifting block: as written, 2 climbs below limit, hangboard 1x or off.
Climbing block: top sets capped @8, 4x3 held at same weight; 3 climbs, one at limit; max hangs before the two hard sessions: 5x7s on 20mm, two in reserve.

## Block calendar
0: Sep 21 - Dec 13 2026, 163->154, finish cut (maintenance week + DEXA Nov 2-8)
1: Dec 14 - Jan 3, 154-155, reverse diet (new training week @RPE 7)
2: Jan 4 - Feb 28, 154->157, climbing (lightest; hangboard starts)
3: Mar 1 - Apr 25, 157->160, lifting (cut's losses come back)
4: Apr 26 - Jun 20, 160->163, climbing (end with outdoor trip or comp)
5: Jun 21 - Aug 15, 163->166, lifting (first block in the 165+ record range)
6: Aug 16 - Oct 10, 166->168, climbing (rate drops to 0.2)
7: Oct 11 - Dec 5, 168->170, lifting (then hold 168-170)
Each block: seven working weeks, light week 4, test week 8. A flat block does not get extended.
Below ~165 you are lighter than any window that produced new strength; blocks 2-4 mostly regain the cut's 15 lb. New barbell ground ~block 5. On the wall it can start in block 2.

## Nutrition
- 0.4 lb/wk (blocks 2-5): maintenance + ~200 kcal/day. 0.2 (6-7): +100.
- Every third Sunday vs the block target line: >1 lb over -> -100; >1 lb under -> +100. Never bigger.
- Protein 0.8-1 g/lb every phase. Carbs onto the four lifting days + limit-climbing day.
- Maintenance week (wk 4): eat at maintenance, not surplus.

## Recomposition variant, active from Dec 14
The year runs on this variant instead of the bulk: same blocks, same training week, same heavy-day rule, but food stays at maintenance and the muscle-building energy comes out of the fat store. Expect ~0.3-0.6 lb muscle/month, a scale that barely moves, body fat at or under 13% all year, climbing that keeps its bodyweight advantage, and roughly 25 lb less on the total by December than the bulk would give, most of that leverage rather than muscle.
- Food: maintenance, 0 to +50; carbs moved onto lifting and limit-climbing days, easy days lower, the week nets to maintenance
- Maintenance: found in the reverse diet, 3 weeks flat within ±0.5 lb; re-found if the scale moves 1.5 lb in 3 weeks
- Protein: 1.0-1.1 g/lb, four feedings, every day
- Weight rate: 0 to +0.15 lb/week; 0.3 for two weeks is the alarm
- Weight band: 152-162, hold 158-161, hard cap 165
- Adjusting: every third Sunday: up more than 1 lb in 3 weeks -> -100; down more than 0.5 -> +100 (a deficit slows the building)
- Fat gauge: waist at the navel, weekly 7-day average; DEXA every 16 weeks (Nov 2026, late Apr, mid Aug, early Dec 2027)
- Muscle gauge: DEXA lean mass every 16 weeks; test-week singles every 8
- If it stalls: two scans with lean mass flat and every input delivered -> +100 kcal for one block, then re-check; not a return to 0.4/week
Everything under Training week, Loads, Block calendar and Tracking still applies. The reverse diet, the first climbing block and the whole cut are unchanged.

## What to track and the switch rules
Five numbers: 7-day avg bodyweight vs the block target line (weekly, weight tab); single @RPE 8 per lift (test week, strength tab); first-time V5+ sends per session (every session, climbing tab); 4x4 speed x incline at equal Zone-4 time (weekly, cardio tab); one-line daily note: fingers, elbows, back, sleep (daily, daily notes — currently empty; this is the gap).
Rules:
- Weight runs >0.6 lb/wk two weeks: take 100 kcal out now.
- Weight flat three weeks: add 100.
- Weight crosses 172: hold at maintenance until next block.
- Lifting block ends flat: inputs delivered (4 days, ~28 working sets, 6 near-max, bench 2x)? Yes -> next lifting block at +0.4. No -> fix inputs, leave food.
- Climbing block ends with no new hard sends: hangboard off next block, add a volume session, check bodyweight vs line.
- Back guarded on a heavy lower day: volume day instead.
- Finger/elbow pain next morning: hangboard off a week, wrist-flexor eccentrics, climb below limit.
- 4x4 work rate or max HR falls two weeks running: light week early.
- Any two in the same week: the week becomes a light week.
