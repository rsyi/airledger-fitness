# Post-cut recomp program — final specification (user-authored, 2026-09-27, supersedes same-day prior version)

Do not change the current cut. Begins only after ~154 lb / ~13% BF and
the maintenance/reverse-diet transition.

## Primary Objective
~12-month maintenance recomp at ~13% BF. Simultaneously: 1. muscle
hypertrophy 2. SBD+OHP strength 3. climbing progression 4. calisthenics
skill 5. VO2 improvement. NOT a conventional bulk; scale weight is not
a progression target.

Expected successful one-year outcome (EXPECTATION RANGES, NOT
controller targets): bw ~158-163; BF ~12-14%; lean +3-6 lb; bench
~260-280; squat ~345-375; deadlift ~370-405; OHP ~150-165; climbing
V6-V7 increasingly consistent, V8 legitimate project territory; VO2
~5-10% greater sustainable 4x4 workload at comparable effort.

## Phase 1 — Maintenance Calibration
~3 weeks establishing true maintenance post-cut. Expect several lb
glycogen/water/food-mass regain; 154 is NOT the maintenance target.
Maintenance = 7d-avg approximately stable 2-3 weeks. Record the
maintenance calorie estimate and glycogen-replete weight baseline.

## Nutrition Targets (~155-160 lb)
Protein 160-175 g/day. Fat minimum ~55-65 g/day. Carbs = remaining
maintenance calories, likely ~225-300 g/day. Calories ~maintenance,
not deliberate surplus. Bias carbs toward lifting/climbing/VO2 days;
~50-75 g carbs 1-3h before demanding sessions as a default.
Body-comp control: 7d-avg weight, weekly waist at navel, periodic
DEXA, performance/strength trends. Do NOT auto-add calories on flat
weight — flat + rising strength/lean mass IS successful recomp.

## Hypertrophy Programming
Explicit tracked objective. 8-12 productive sets per major muscle/wk
(~10 default). Count meaningful overlap from compounds, climbing,
calisthenics — do not force 10 lifting sets for back/biceps on top of
climbing. Most hypertrophy work RPE 7-9 / ~1-3 RIR; compounds ~2-3
RIR; safe isolations occasionally ~0-1 RIR. Track weekly productive
sets by muscle group; flag BOTH obvious underdosing and excessive
overlapping volume.

## Strength Periodization — repeating ~4-week wave (S/B/D/OHP)
Week 1: top ~5 @ RPE 7-8. Week 2: top ~3 @ RPE 7-8. Week 3: top ~1 @
RPE 7-8. Week 4: deload/reduced volume. Continue Ledger's working-max
controller for loading. Avoid routine AMRAP/grinders. Normal weeks:
heavy work followed by hypertrophy backoffs, generally 3-4 sets of
~5-8 @ ~1-3 RIR. Deadlift supplemental volume may stay lower (fatigue
cost). Accessories: double progression (all sets at top of rep range
at target RIR -> increase load).

## Longer Periodization — ~8-week emphasis blocks
Lifting-emphasis: 4 lifting exposures/wk; 8-12 sets/muscle/wk;
climbing 2x; calisthenics skill 1x; 4x4 1x.
Climbing/calisthenics-emphasis: reduce lifting volume ~30-40%,
PRESERVE heavy exposures; climbing may go 3x; more focused
calisthenics; 4x4 stays 1x. Never completely turn off any major
adaptation during an emphasis block.

## Default Weekly Schedule
Mon — Squat + Lower + Muscle-Up: MU 3-5 x 1-2 skill quality; squat
top work per periodization; squat backoffs ~3x5-8; Romanian deadlift
3x6-8; Bulgarian split squat 2x8-12/leg; hanging leg raise 3x8-15.
Tue — Bench + Technique Climbing: paused bench top work; bench
backoffs ~3x5-8; seated cable row 3x6-10; lateral raise 3x10-20;
triceps extension 2x8-15; face pull 2x12-20; external rotation
2x12-20 easy; climbing technique/volume ~V3-V5, onsight +
movement-quality focus. Avoid unnecessary extra pulling (climbing
supplies substantial pulling/finger volume).
Wed — Calisthenics Skill (~30-45 min): handstand practice; HSPU
progression 3-4 quality sets; front-lever progression 3-4 quality
sets; pistol squat 3x3-6/leg; optional core. Skill quality over
fatigue; well short of failure.
Thu — VO2: Norwegian 4x4 (warmup, 4x4min hard, ~3min recovery,
cooldown). Track workload, Z4/Z5 exposure, HR. Purpose is
cardiovascular adaptation, not calories.
Fri — Deadlift + Limit Climbing: deadlift top work; deadlift backoffs
~2x4-6; leg press/hack squat 3x8-12; leg curl 3x8-15; calf raise
3x8-15; limit climbing V5-V7+ projects, long rests, low junk volume.
Separate lifting and climbing by several hours when practical.
Sat — OHP + Upper Hypertrophy: OHP top work; OHP backoffs ~3x5-8;
bench 3x6-10; seated cable row 3x8-12; lateral raise 3x12-20;
EZ-bar/preacher curl 2-3x8-15; triceps extension 2-3x8-15; face pull
2x12-20; external rotation 2x12-20.
Sun — Recovery: no hard training; walking/easy activity encouraged;
optional easy Zone 2 if recovery is good.

## Volume Ramp (post-cut)
Maintenance week 1: ~60-70% planned accessory volume. Week 2:
~80-90%. Week 3 onward: full volume if recovery adequate.

## Progression / Adjustment Principle
Training progression, not scale weight, is the primary signal. Before
increasing calories, determine whether a plateau is caused by:
inadequate volume; inadequate proximity to failure; poor progressive
overload; insufficient carbs; poor sleep/recovery; excessive
climbing/calisthenics overlap; inconsistent adherence. If ALL inputs
are consistently adequate and progress remains stalled: ~+100 kcal/day
for one block, reassess. Maintain ~13% BF throughout — no traditional
bulk/cut cycle.
